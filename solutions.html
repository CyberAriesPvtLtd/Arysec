DPO as a Service

An appointed, qualified Data Protection Officer who discharges your statutory privacy obligations — under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU GDPR, and sector-specific regulation — without adding permanent headcount.

We act as your named point of contact for regulators and data principals, maintain your privacy documentation, and keep your processing defensible as your business changes.

Discuss a DPO appointment →

What's Included

  • Named DPO appointment and regulator liaison
  • Data mapping and records of processing activities

  • Data subject and data principal request handling
  • Breach assessment and statutory notification
  • Cross-border transfer assessment
  • Privacy training and awareness programmes

Compliance

Frameworks We Take You Through

From first gap analysis to certification and the surveillance audits that follow.

ISO/IEC 27001

End-to-end ISMS implementation — scoping, risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, Annex A controls, internal audit, and certification support.

SOC 2

Type I and Type II readiness across the Trust Services Criteria, including control design, evidence collection, and auditor coordination.

DPDP Act, 2023

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act — obligations of data fiduciaries, consent architecture, and data principal rights.

EU GDPR

Lawful basis, records of processing, DPIAs, transfer mechanisms, and DPO representation for organizations serving EU markets.

PCI DSS

Cardholder data environment scoping, control implementation, and readiness for businesses processing payment data.

NIST CSF

Maturity assessment and roadmap development against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework functions and categories.

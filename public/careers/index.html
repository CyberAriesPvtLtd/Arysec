Working Here

What Working Here Looks Like

Arysec Consultancy LLP is an IT and information security firm based in Mumbai, with a team of more than fifty certified professionals serving clients across India and international markets. We run security audits, governance programmes, security operations and IT services, which means the person sitting next to you may be closing an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 gap register while you finish a web application test.

We hire for judgement and curiosity ahead of a specific tool list. Certifications matter to us and we support people through them, but we care more about whether you can explain a finding to the client who has to fund the fix. New joiners are paired with a senior consultant, given real scope early, and reviewed honestly rather than politely.

Six roles are open at the moment, across security operations, offensive security, governance, privacy, cloud and managed IT. Some are on site in Mumbai, some hybrid, one fully remote within India. If none of them fits and you still think you would be useful here, write to careers@arysec.in and tell us what you would want to work on.

Ownership of Engagements

You are named on the work you deliver. Consultants run their own scopes, write their own reports and present findings to the client rather than handing them upward.

Evidence Over Opinion

Findings need proof. We expect reproducible steps, screenshots and clear risk ratings, and we push back internally on anything a client could reasonably dispute in a review meeting.

Learning as Routine

Time is set aside for research, lab work and certification study. Senior consultants review juniors' reports line by line, which is slower than fixing them quietly and works better.

Straight Talk

Bad news travels fast here. If a test slips or a control will not pass, we tell the client early and we say the same thing internally.

Benefits

What We Offer

Certification Support

We sponsor role-relevant certifications such as CEH, OSCP, CISA, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor and cloud credentials, with study time agreed before you book the exam.

Hybrid Working

Consulting and engineering roles work hybrid from our Mumbai office, with fully remote arrangements for some positions. Operations roles that need the SOC floor stay on site.

Lab and Licences

You get a testing lab, cloud sandbox accounts and licences for the tooling your role needs, not a first look at it mid-engagement.

Mentoring and Review

Every new joiner is paired with a senior consultant for the first engagements, and review conversations happen on a set cycle rather than only at appraisal time.

Cross-Practice Exposure

Analysts move into testing, testers move into cloud work and consultants pick up audit scope. Internal moves are discussed openly rather than treated as a resignation risk.

Client Exposure Early

You sit in client meetings from your first months, present your own findings and learn how a CISO or a finance head actually hears risk.

You work on our security operations floor, triaging alerts from client SIEM and EDR platforms across banking, SaaS and manufacturing environments. The role runs on a rotating shift pattern, including nights, and suits an analyst who wants depth in detection rather than ticket volume.

What you will do

  • Triage and investigate SIEM and EDR alerts, escalating confirmed incidents with a clear timeline and impact assessment.

You test web applications, APIs, mobile apps, internal networks and cloud environments for clients in financial services, SaaS and healthcare. Scopes are yours end to end: planning, testing, reporting and the retest after fixes land. Some engagements require travel to client sites within India.

Hiring Process

How We Hire

01

Application Review

Send your CV to careers@arysec.in with the role title in the subject line. We read every application ourselves and reply either way, usually within a week.

02

First Conversation

A call with the practice lead covering your experience, the role scope and what you want next. Ask your questions here; we would rather you did.

03

Practical Exercise

We set a task drawn from real work: a short test scope, an alert triage, a control gap or a support scenario. Reasoning matters more than polish.

04

Offer and Onboarding

Final discussion on scope, expectations and terms, then a structured first month with a named mentor, shadowed engagements and your own scope by the end of it.

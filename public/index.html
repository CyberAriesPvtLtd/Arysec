ISO 27001 Certification for a B2B SaaS Provider

Enterprise deals were stalling in security review, and prospects had begun making ISO/IEC 27001 certification a condition of signing. The company had scattered policies, no risk register anyone maintained, and a security questionnaire process that consumed engineering time every month. Leadership wanted certification within the financial year without pulling the product team off the roadmap.

Healthcare & Diagnostics

A Named DPO for a Diagnostics Network

Health data moved between collection centres, a laboratory information system, a consumer app and several pathology partners, and nobody could describe the full picture in one document. The board had read enough about the DPDP Act 2023 to be uneasy about consent, retention and grievance handling, but there was no privacy owner and no process for answering a data principal who wrote in.

NBFC & Digital Lending

Building an IT Internal Audit Programme at an NBFC

Internal audit existed on paper but ran as an annual scramble ahead of the board meeting, with findings written broadly enough that nobody had to own them. IT and information security were barely covered. The audit committee wanted a programme with real coverage of access management, change control, outsourcing and the lending application, and evidence that issues actually closed.

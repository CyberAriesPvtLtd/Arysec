Flagship Solution

vCISO as a Service

Most growing organisations reach a point where security decisions outrun the people available to make them. Customers send long security questionnaires, an insurer asks to see a risk register, the board wants to know whether the spend is proportionate, and the IT manager is already fully committed to keeping systems running. A full-time chief information security officer is one answer. A fractional one is the other.

Our vCISO engagement places a senior practitioner inside your organisation for an agreed number of days each month. That person owns the security strategy and roadmap, maintains the risk register, drafts the policy framework and takes it through approval, and represents security to your board, your auditors and your customers. They are accountable for the programme, not merely available for advice.

Flagship Solution

DPO as a Service

The DPDP Act 2023 places duties on every Data Fiduciary, and organisations the Central Government notifies as Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint a Data Protection Officer based in India, answerable to the board or an equivalent governing body, who acts as the contact point for grievance redressal. Under the EU GDPR, Article 37 requires a designation where an organisation is a public body, where core activities involve regular and systematic monitoring of individuals on a large scale, or where they involve large-scale processing of special category or criminal conviction data. Whether either trigger applies to you is a question of fact, and we recommend you confirm your position with counsel.

The work is continuous rather than occasional. Someone has to keep the record of processing activities current, decide when a data protection impact assessment is required, test the lawful basis behind each purpose, answer access and erasure requests inside statutory deadlines, judge whether an incident meets a notification threshold, and stay across cross-border transfer arrangements as products and vendors change. Article 39 of the GDPR sets out those tasks in list form; the DPDP Act reaches similar ground through fiduciary duties and the additional obligations placed on Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Flagship Solution

Internal Audit & Assurance

Internal audit sits in a different place from the two things it is most often confused with. The statutory audit is bounded by company law and speaks to shareholders. A penetration test asks whether a particular system can be broken into on a particular day. Internal audit answers a broader question for your board: are the controls management says exist actually designed correctly and operating over a period, across finance, operations, technology and compliance.

We provide that function on an outsourced basis, where we are your internal audit team, or co-sourced, where we work alongside your in-house auditors and bring the technology and analytics skills that are hardest to recruit. Either way the work is planned against risk rather than habit. The annual plan is built from your risk register, your regulatory obligations, prior findings and the places where the organisation has recently changed — a new ERP, an acquisition, a move to cloud hosting, a large outsourcing arrangement.

Security Built Around Your Sector

Threats, regulators and operating constraints differ by industry. We bring the sector context to the engagement rather than learning it on your time, and we recommend you confirm your own regulatory position with counsel.

Banking & Financial Services

Banks, NBFCs, cooperative banks, payment aggregators and fintech lenders operate under close supervisory attention, handle money movement in real time, and carry outsourcing chains that regulators expect you to govern as carefully as your own systems.

What we see in this sector

  • Account takeover and mule networks driven by credential stuffing, SIM swap and social engineering.
  • Third-party and API risk across lending service providers, co-lending partners and core banking vendors.
  • Incident reporting timelines that leave little room for slow detection or unclear escalation paths.
  • Legacy core systems and branch networks that resist patching, segmentation and modern authentication.
RBI IT governance and cyber resilience directions SEBI Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework PCI DSS 4.0 DPDP Act 2023 ISO/IEC 27001:2022

How we help

Supervisory Readiness Reviews

We map your controls against the RBI expectations that apply to your entity class, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and internal audit scope, then hand you a gap register with owners and dates.

Payment Stack Testing

Application, API and network testing across net banking, UPI and card flows, run against OWASP ASVS and PCI DSS 4.0 requirements, with retesting included after your fixes land.

Fraud Detection Engineering

Detection content tuned for financial abuse patterns in your SIEM, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, so account takeover and unusual payment behaviour raise an alert someone actually owns.

Outsourcing Governance

Vendor risk tiering, security clauses for service provider contracts, and periodic assessment of the partners handling your customer data, so oversight of the chain is documented.

Insurance

Insurers, brokers and third-party administrators hold health records, financial detail and claims history in one place. That concentration, plus long-lived policy data and a wide intermediary network, shapes every security decision you make.

What we see in this sector

  • Claims fraud and identity misuse enabled by weak verification at intermediary and agent portals.
  • Sensitive health and financial data retained for decades across policy administration and legacy archives.
  • Large intermediary networks with shared logins, personal devices and uneven security awareness.
  • Digital claims journeys and partner integrations that expose APIs to unauthenticated data access.
IRDAI Information and Cyber Security Guidelines DPDP Act 2023 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 SOC 2

How we help

Data Discovery and Classification

We locate policyholder and health data across core systems, shared drives and archives, classify it, and set retention and access rules you can defend to a regulator.

Intermediary Access Control

Identity and access management for agents, brokers and administrators: unique accounts, multi-factor authentication, least privilege on policy data, and periodic recertification with an audit trail.

Claims Platform Testing

Security testing of claims portals, customer apps and partner APIs, covering authorisation flaws that let one policyholder reach another's records, with fixes verified on retest.

Breach Response Planning

Playbooks, a tested escalation path and rehearsed notification steps for the IRDAI and DPDP timelines your legal team confirms, so the first hour is not improvised.

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Hospitals, diagnostic chains, digital health platforms and pharmaceutical firms combine clinical uptime pressure with some of the most sensitive personal data there is, alongside connected medical devices nobody wants taken offline for patching.

What we see in this sector

  • Ransomware against hospital information systems, where downtime becomes a clinical safety problem within hours.
  • Medical devices and imaging equipment running unsupported operating systems on flat clinical networks.
  • Research and trial data shared with sponsors, CROs and academic partners across borders.
  • Patient portals and health apps handling records that carry lifelong consequences if exposed.
DPDP Act 2023 HIPAA (US-facing work) EU GDPR ISO/IEC 27001:2022 SOC 2

How we help

Clinical Network Segmentation

We separate medical devices, clinical workstations and administrative IT so an infected billing machine cannot reach an imaging modality, using compensating controls where patching is not permitted.

Ransomware Recovery Testing

Backup design, immutability checks and a restore exercise against your hospital information system, so recovery time is a measured figure rather than a vendor's promise.

Privacy Programme Build

Consent, purpose limitation, retention and data principal request handling built for clinical reality, aligned to the DPDP Act 2023 and, where you act for US covered entities or serve patients in the EU, the HIPAA or GDPR duties your counsel confirms.

Third-Party Clinical Vendors

Assessment of labs, billing partners, teleradiology providers and cloud EMR vendors, with security terms in contracts and a review cycle proportionate to the data each one touches.

Technology & SaaS

Software companies sell to buyers whose procurement teams audit them. Your security posture is a revenue dependency, tested by questionnaires, penetration test reports and certification evidence long before a contract is signed.

What we see in this sector

  • Enterprise security questionnaires and vendor reviews stalling deals late in the sales cycle.
  • Multi-tenant architecture where a single authorisation flaw exposes one customer's data to another.
  • Cloud misconfiguration and over-permissive IAM roles accumulating faster than anyone reviews them.
  • Supply chain exposure through open-source dependencies, build pipelines and third-party integrations.
SOC 2 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 DPDP Act 2023 EU GDPR PCI DSS 4.0

How we help

Certification Readiness

We prepare you for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 or SOC 2 audits: scope, gap analysis, control implementation, evidence collection and auditor liaison, so the audit examines controls that are already running.

Questionnaire and Diligence Support

Your sales team gets a maintained answer library, a trust summary and a security lead who joins prospect calls, so procurement reviews stop landing on engineering.

Application and API Testing

Grey-box testing against OWASP Top 10 and ASVS, with tenant isolation and authorisation logic treated as first-class test cases, plus SAST and DAST wired into your pipeline.

Cloud Posture Management

Configuration review of AWS, Azure or GCP against CIS Benchmarks, identity permissions trimmed to what workloads use, and CSPM tuned so alerts reflect real risk.

Manufacturing

Plants run equipment older than the security tools meant to protect it. Production lines cannot pause for patching, engineering data is worth stealing, and IT and OT networks are often more connected than the drawings suggest.

What we see in this sector

  • Legacy PLCs, SCADA and HMI systems that cannot be patched without a production window.
  • Flat plant networks where an office phishing email reaches shop floor controllers.
  • Intellectual property theft targeting designs, formulations, tooling data and process know-how.
  • Remote access for equipment vendors, often through shared credentials and unmanaged tools.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 IEC 62443 NIST CSF 2.0 DPDP Act 2023

How we help

OT Asset Visibility

Passive discovery of what is actually on the plant network, including controllers and engineering workstations nobody documented, producing an asset inventory that survives the next audit.

IT and OT Segmentation

Zone and conduit design informed by IEC 62443 thinking, with a controlled boundary between business systems and production, planned around shutdown windows rather than against them.

Vendor Remote Access

Brokered, time-bound and recorded access for equipment suppliers replaces standing VPN accounts and shared passwords, so you know who touched which machine and when.

Continuity and Recovery

Backups for engineering configurations and controller logic, plus a recovery plan rehearsed with plant staff, because restoring a line matters more than restoring a file server.

Retail & E-Commerce

Card data, loyalty balances and buying histories sit behind storefronts built for speed. Peak season concentrates risk, payment rules apply whether you store card data or not, and a checkout outage is visible to everyone.

What we see in this sector

  • Payment page skimming and malicious scripts injected through third-party tags and plugins.
  • Credential stuffing against customer accounts, loyalty points and stored payment instruments.
  • Bot traffic, scalping and fraud spikes that arrive with sale events and festive peaks.
  • Point of sale terminals, store networks and franchise sites managed by non-technical staff.
PCI DSS 4.0 DPDP Act 2023 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 EU GDPR

How we help

PCI DSS 4.0 Readiness

Scoping, gap assessment and control work across the cardholder data environment, including the client-side script controls that catch many merchants out, ahead of your assessor's visit.

Storefront Security Testing

Testing of web and mobile commerce flows: checkout, coupon and refund logic, account recovery, and the APIs your apps and marketplace integrations depend on.

Peak Season Monitoring

Extra monitoring cover and an agreed escalation path across your sale periods, with detection tuned for fraud and availability abuse rather than generic alerting.

Store and Franchise Hardening

Standard builds for point of sale devices, network separation between till systems and guest Wi-Fi, and a checklist store managers can follow without a technical background.

Logistics & Supply Chain

Freight forwarders, 3PL operators and warehouse networks run systems that customers plan around. Downtime stops trucks, partner integrations multiply the attack surface, and invoice fraud follows the money moving through your bookings.

What we see in this sector

  • Uptime sensitivity where an hour of system downtime becomes missed pickups and penalty clauses.
  • EDI and API links to shippers, carriers and customs brokers, each a trust boundary.
  • Business email compromise redirecting freight payments through altered invoice and bank details.
  • Warehouse and yard technology, handheld scanners and telematics sitting outside standard IT management.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 DPDP Act 2023 SOC 2 EU GDPR

How we help

Resilience and Availability

We design for degraded operation: redundancy where it counts, tested failover for booking and warehouse management systems, and recovery objectives agreed with operations rather than assumed by IT.

Partner Integration Security

Authentication, authorisation and monitoring for EDI feeds, carrier APIs and customer portals, so a compromised partner account does not become free movement across your platform.

Payment Fraud Controls

Email authentication, mailbox rule monitoring and a verification step for bank detail changes, backed by training for the finance and operations staff who approve payments.

Customer Assurance Support

Large shippers audit their logistics providers. We prepare the evidence, close the gaps and support you through ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification audits and customer security reviews.

Professional Services

Law firms, accountancy practices, consultancies and agencies hold client material they did not create and cannot afford to lose. Client confidentiality clauses, not statute alone, often set the security bar you must meet.

What we see in this sector

  • Client confidentiality obligations and engagement terms that impose security duties beyond regulation.
  • Partner and consultant devices moving between client sites, home networks and travel.
  • Email as the primary channel for sensitive documents, with attachments forwarded widely.
  • Small internal IT teams supporting fee earners who resist anything that slows work.
DPDP Act 2023 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 SOC 2 EU GDPR Client contractual security terms

How we help

Client Data Segregation

Matter-based or client-based access boundaries in your document systems, so information walls are enforced by permissions and logging instead of professional habit alone.

Device and Identity Controls

Managed endpoints, full disk encryption, conditional access and phishing-resistant authentication configured to interfere as little as possible with how partners and consultants actually work.

Assurance for Client Audits

Completed security questionnaires, a summarised control position and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 readiness work, so enterprise clients and their procurement teams get answers without pulling partners off billable work.

Targeted Awareness Training

Short, role-relevant sessions and simulated phishing aimed at the people who handle client funds and documents, with reporting your management committee can read in minutes.

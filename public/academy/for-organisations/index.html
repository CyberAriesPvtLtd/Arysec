Start With the Exposure, Not the Catalogue

Buying training by the course usually produces a schedule that satisfies a budget line and misses the actual risk. We start the other way round: what would hurt this organisation, who is in a position to prevent it, and what is the smallest programme that changes their behaviour.

For most organisations that means awareness and phishing drills for everyone, one technical track for the team that builds or runs the systems, and a leadership briefing so the people funding the programme understand what they are funding. Certification tracks come in when there is a specific compliance driver.

Programmes are quoted per cohort rather than per head, so training the whole team costs the same as training half of it. That is deliberate: per-seat pricing is why organisations train the people who can spare the time rather than the people who need it.

What We Tailor

Tailoring is included in cohort pricing rather than charged as an extra. Generic material is the single biggest reason training gets ignored.

  • Screenshots and walkthroughs using your actual systems and tools
  • Phishing pretexts drawn from your sector, region and calendar
  • Labs built in your language, framework and cloud platform
  • Scenarios rebuilt from your own incidents and near-misses
  • Policy references pointed at your documents, not generic examples
  • Regulatory content scoped to the obligations that apply to you
  • Delivery in English or Hindi, with other languages on request

Formats

Delivery Formats

Most annual programmes mix two or three of these rather than committing to one.

On-Site Cohorts

At your office anywhere in India. Best for leadership briefings, tabletop exercises and any session where the discussion between attendees matters as much as the content.

Live Online

Same instructor, same exercises, cohorts of up to 40 for awareness and 16 for labs. Suits distributed teams and avoids losing a day to travel.

Self-Paced Modules

For shift workers, field staff, new joiners and anyone a scheduled session never reaches. Delivered through your LMS or ours, with completion records either way.

Blended Programmes

A live core session, self-paced reinforcement, and quarterly drills. This is what most annual programmes settle into after the first cycle.

Train the Trainer

For larger organisations running internal delivery: your facilitators are trained on our material and licensed to deliver it, with content updates included.

Executive Briefings

Ninety-minute sessions timed to fit a board or leadership meeting, delivered on your agenda rather than requiring a separate diary slot.

Reporting

Records and Reporting

Everything below is produced as a matter of course, not quoted as an add-on.

Attendance and Completion

Per-employee records exportable as CSV, structured for an ISO 27001 or SOC 2 evidence pack without reformatting.

Assessment Results

For certification tracks, results and certificate references per delegate, with verification codes your HR team can check.

Behavioural Metrics

Click rate, report rate and time to first report from phishing drills, trended across the year rather than reported per campaign.

Management Summary

A one-page view after each cycle: what moved, what did not, which teams need attention and what the next cycle should target.

LMS Integration

Self-paced material delivered as SCORM packages into your existing LMS, or hosted by us if you would rather not run one.

Data Handling

Delegate data is processed under a defined retention period, individual phishing results are never published internally, and a processing agreement is signed before any data moves.

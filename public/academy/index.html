Why We Built It

Training Fails When Nobody Measures Behaviour

Almost every organisation buys security training, and most of it is watched at double speed the week before an audit. Completion percentages go up, an obligation is discharged, and nothing about the way people work actually changes.

We built the academy because the same gap kept showing up in our consulting work. The control was documented, the training was recorded, and an invoice still got paid to an attacker's account. What was missing was relevance, reinforcement and any measurement of what people did rather than what they clicked through.

Every programme here is taught by practitioners who do the work — the people running ISO 27001 implementations, penetration tests and incident response for Arysec clients. The examples come from those engagements, and the material changes when what we are seeing changes.

Phishing Drills

Find Out How Your Staff Respond Before an Attacker Does

A phishing drill is a fire drill. We send controlled, realistic messages using the pretexts your sector is actually seeing, and measure two things: how many people clicked, and how many reported. The second number is the one that decides how a real incident goes.

Anyone who clicks gets a thirty-second explanation at the moment it matters, never a place on a list. Leadership gets a one-page trend, and the security team gets the detail — including how long it took before the first person raised the alarm.

How Drills Work

What a Campaign Measures

Every wave produces the same set of numbers, so the trend across a year means something.

  • Pretexts drawn from live campaigns against your sector and region
  • Click rate, credential submission and report rate, tracked over time
  • Median time to first report — your real detection latency
  • Targeted waves for finance, payroll and anyone who can move money
  • No captured passwords, no league tables, no blame
  • Board-ready reporting after every campaign

How It Runs

From First Conversation to a Programme That Repeats

Most engagements start with a single cohort and grow into an annual plan once the first set of numbers exists.

Scope

We look at your sector, your stack, your obligations and any recent near-misses, then recommend the smallest programme that addresses the real exposure.

02

03

One Control Set, Many Frameworks

Compliance rarely arrives one framework at a time. A payments partner asks for PCI DSS 4.0, a US enterprise buyer wants a SOC 2 Type II report, your regulator expects controls aligned to its cyber security directions, and somewhere in the middle a healthcare client raises HIPAA. Treated as four separate projects, each one repeats the same work: the same access reviews, the same change records, the same vendor assessments, collected four times by four different people.

We run compliance as a single programme. Your obligations are mapped onto one control set, so a documented quarterly access review satisfies the SOC 2 logical access criteria, the PCI DSS requirement for least privilege and the corresponding NIST CSF 2.0 outcome at the same time. Where frameworks genuinely diverge, and they do, we scope the difference explicitly rather than pretending a generic policy covers it.

Be clear about what we are and are not. Arysec does the readiness work: scoping, control design, internal testing, evidence assembly and sitting beside you through fieldwork. The opinion itself always comes from an independent firm, because the rules that give an attestation its value stop whoever designed your controls from also testing them for the report. We treat that split as a discipline rather than a limitation, since it forces our internal testing to be harder than the assessor's. Obligations for entities supervised by the Reserve Bank of India, SEBI or IRDAI are revised periodically and differ by licence class, so your compliance counsel should confirm where yours leaves you.

Capabilities

Frameworks and Assessment Work

SOC 2 Readiness

We scope the Trust Services Criteria that apply, design controls for Type I, then run the observation window with monitored evidence collection so your Type II period does not fail on a missed sample.

PCI DSS 4.0 Scoping

Cardholder data flows are traced end to end, assessment scope is reduced where segmentation or tokenisation allows, and each requirement is tested against the defined approach or a documented customised implementation.

NIST CSF 2.0 Maturity

Current and target profiles are scored across all six functions, including Govern, giving your board a maturity view that is comparable year on year and maps cleanly onto other frameworks.

Indian Regulatory Alignment

Regulated entities under the Reserve Bank of India, SEBI or IRDAI face sector-specific cyber security and outsourcing expectations. We read the directions that apply to your licence class and align controls, reporting and testing accordingly.

Gap Analysis and Remediation

Every control is assessed twice: is it designed to meet the requirement, and is it operating as designed. Findings are rated by audit risk and issued with owners, effort estimates and target dates.

Evidence and Auditor Liaison

Evidence requests are answered from a maintained repository rather than assembled under pressure. We manage the assessor's sample requests, walkthrough scheduling and follow-up questions, and we push back where a request exceeds agreed scope.

How It Works

Our Engagement Process

How a Audit & Compliance engagement runs from first conversation to steady state.

01

Boundary

We establish which frameworks genuinely apply, then draw the boundary: which entities, systems, data flows and third parties are in. Getting this wrong is the most common cause of audit overruns.

02

Gap Register

Interviews, configuration review, document inspection and sample testing produce a rated gap register. You learn early which findings are quick policy fixes and which need engineering time or budget approval.

03

Close Out

Your team closes findings with our support: control design, policy drafting, tooling configuration and evidence workflows. We retest each item and hold a formal readiness review before you engage the external assessor.

04

Fieldwork

We sit alongside you through fieldwork, managing sample requests and walkthroughs, then set the recurring calendar of access reviews, risk assessments and vendor checks that keeps the next cycle shorter.

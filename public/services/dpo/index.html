The Role, Filled Properly

The DPDP Act 2023 places duties on every Data Fiduciary, and organisations the Central Government notifies as Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint a Data Protection Officer based in India, answerable to the board or an equivalent governing body, who acts as the contact point for grievance redressal. Under the EU GDPR, Article 37 requires a designation where an organisation is a public body, where core activities involve regular and systematic monitoring of individuals on a large scale, or where they involve large-scale processing of special category or criminal conviction data. Whether either trigger applies to you is a question of fact, and we recommend you confirm your position with counsel.

The work is continuous rather than occasional. Someone has to keep the record of processing activities current, decide when a data protection impact assessment is required, test the lawful basis behind each purpose, answer access and erasure requests inside statutory deadlines, judge whether an incident meets a notification threshold, and stay across cross-border transfer arrangements as products and vendors change. Article 39 of the GDPR sets out those tasks in list form; the DPDP Act reaches similar ground through fiduciary duties and the additional obligations placed on Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Article 37(6) of the GDPR allows the role to be filled on the basis of a service contract, and there is a structural reason to do so. Article 38(6) bars other duties that create a conflict of interests, which makes a head of IT, marketing or human resources awkward to appoint: each of them decides how personal data gets used. An appointed external officer does not set purposes or means, so the independence the law expects is easier to evidence. Our reporting line is to your board, not to the function under review.

The Privacy Record Set

Documents you can hand to a regulator, an auditor or an enterprise customer's privacy team, kept current rather than written once and filed.

  • Record of processing activities, maintained per business function
  • Data flow maps tracing each transfer route end to end
  • Privacy notices, consent artefacts and withdrawal journeys
  • DPIA reports with residual risk and treatment decisions
  • Cross-border transfer inventory with safeguard mechanisms
  • Rights request procedure, response templates and case log
  • Breach assessment playbook and statutory notification drafts
  • Role-based privacy training material and completion records

Capabilities

What The Role Covers

Appointment And Contact Point

We name a qualified officer, publish the contact details your privacy notice must carry, and act as the point of contact for regulators, data principals and grievance escalations.

Data Mapping And RoPA

Interviews with process owners, system inventories and discovery tooling produce a record of processing activities per Article 30: purposes, categories, recipients, retention periods, transfers and safeguards, maintained as a living register.

Consent And Lawful Basis

Every processing purpose gets a documented basis. We review notice wording, consent capture and withdrawal journeys, children's data handling, and the record you would need to show an adjudicating authority.

Impact Assessments

Screening criteria decide which projects need a DPIA. We run the assessment against ISO/IEC 29134 structure, document residual risk, and advise when prior consultation with a supervisory authority is warranted.

Rights And Grievance Handling

A workflow for access, correction, erasure, portability and objection: identity verification, search across systems, exemption analysis, redaction of third-party data, and a defensible response inside the statutory clock.

Breach Assessment And Notification

When an incident touches personal data we assess severity, decide whether thresholds are met, draft the regulator filing and the notice to affected individuals, and keep the internal breach register.

How It Works

Our Engagement Process

How a DPO engagement runs from first conversation to steady state.

01

Scoping

We establish which regimes apply, whether a designation is mandatory or voluntary, and where your processing sits today. The output is a scope note and a prioritised gap list.

02

Mapping

Workshops with product, engineering, marketing, human resources and finance produce the processing inventory. We trace each data set to its purpose, lawful basis, retention period, processors and transfer route.

03

Remediation

Gaps get owners and dates. Typical work includes rewriting notices, fixing consent capture, tightening processor contracts, building the rights request workflow, and running the first round of privacy training.

04

Standing Duty

From then on the officer is on call: DPIAs for new projects, rights requests, incident assessment, vendor reviews, regulator correspondence, and a quarterly report to your board or audit committee.

Benefits

What You Get Out of It

Independence You Can Show

An external officer does not set the purposes and means of processing, so the conflict of interests bar in Article 38(6) is straightforward to satisfy and evidence.

Depth Without A Headcount

You get privacy law, security and engineering perspectives from a team rather than one hire, at a fixed monthly fee scoped to your processing footprint.

Faster Enterprise Deals

Procurement and vendor security questionnaires ask who your DPO is and to see your RoPA. Having named answers and current documents shortens the review cycle.

Continuity Through Change

Cover does not lapse when an individual resigns or goes on leave. A backup officer knows your estate, and the documentation stays with you either way.

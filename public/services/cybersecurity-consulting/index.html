Decisions First, Tools Later

Security spending in most organisations accumulates rather than gets decided. A tool arrives after an incident, another after an audit finding, a third because a vendor made a persuasive case to somebody in the room. Two years on, the leadership team can list what it owns but cannot say which risks are covered, which are being accepted by default, and whether the next increment of budget belongs in identity, detection or recovery.

This engagement answers that question with evidence rather than opinion. We identify the assets whose loss, alteration or exposure would genuinely hurt, model the routes an attacker would take to reach them, measure the controls you operate today against a published framework, and express the result as ranked risk instead of a list of findings. Each recommendation carries a cost, an owner and a stated consequence if it is deferred.

Assessment work is kept separate from implementation work for a reason. The honest answer is sometimes that you should consolidate tools you already own, change how a control is operated, or accept a risk formally and move on. Where new investment is justified we describe the capability required and the criteria to evaluate it against, and leave procurement with you. What you keep is a position you can defend to your board, your insurer and your regulator.

Capabilities

Inside the Risk Assessment

Asset and Crown-Jewel Mapping

We work with business owners to identify the data, systems and processes whose loss would actually stop revenue or trigger regulatory notice, then trace the accounts, networks and suppliers that reach them.

Threat Modelling

Threat scenarios are built around your sector and architecture using MITRE ATT&CK techniques and STRIDE analysis, so the risks discussed are ones plausibly reachable by adversaries who target organisations like yours.

Maturity Benchmarking

Current practice is scored function by function against NIST CSF 2.0 or CIS Controls v8.1, with evidence behind each rating, giving you a baseline that can be re-measured in later years.

Security Architecture Review

Segmentation, privileged access paths, log coverage, endpoint controls and cloud configuration are examined as one connected design rather than as separate purchases, because the gaps usually sit between the products.

Zero-Trust Strategy

Where a zero-trust direction makes sense, we map your current state against NIST SP 800-207, sequence the identity, device and segmentation work realistically, and say plainly which parts your estate cannot yet support.

Risk Quantification and Appetite

Findings are translated into loss exposure using a defined method such as FAIR or ISO/IEC 27005 scoring, and tested against a risk appetite statement agreed with your leadership team.

How It Works

Our Engagement Process

How a Security Consulting engagement runs from first conversation to steady state.

01

Frame

We agree the scope, the business services in question and the decisions this assessment must inform, then interview executives to capture what the organisation already believes about its own exposure.

02

Assess

Workshops, control testing, configuration review and document sampling run in parallel over an agreed window. Where evidence contradicts what we were told, we verify it before it reaches a report.

03

Quantify

Gaps become risk scenarios with likelihood, impact and existing mitigations recorded. Each one is reviewed with the accountable business owner so the ranking reflects operational reality rather than our judgement alone.

04

Decide

Findings are presented twice: once to the technical team for challenge and correction, once to leadership as a set of funding and sequencing choices. You leave that session with a signed-off roadmap.
